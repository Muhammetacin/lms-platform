# LMS-097 — Course Assignment Administration Screen

Epic: Course Assignments (P5)

- Link to the assignments screen from a published course's management page.
- Search employees and teams, choose a target, and grant the course. Display existing assignments and their target type and availability.
- Confirm revocation and give accessible feedback for successful mutations, loading and errors.
- The responsive administrator view performs all changes through the authorized LMS-096 API.
